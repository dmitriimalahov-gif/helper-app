Data used

Accounts use your name, email, protected password hash, language and settings. Voluntarily linking Telegram adds your Telegram identifier and profile information available to the bot. A phone number is used if you provide it.

Your diary contains the vehicle, mileage, maintenance, expense, fuel, document expiry and reminder details you enter. Synchronization sends those records to the server. Receipt photos stay on your device and are excluded from synchronization. Vehicle-document photo recognition in the web app runs on the device; fields you confirm are saved.

Offer and partner features may store company recommendations, visits, ratings, coupons, achievements and offer-view events. Technical records include IP addresses, request times, sessions and security events; payment records include transaction identifiers and subscription status.

Purposes and grounds

Account and diary data provide the functions you request and fulfill the service terms. Session and error information protects accounts and system operation under the operator's legitimate interests. Relevant records may also be processed to meet legal obligations.

News and location offers require separate consent, which you can withdraw in settings. The bot supports /news_off and /geo_off; /stop stops bot messages. Location requires device permission. The optional address lookup sends coordinates to Nominatim/OpenStreetMap. Aggregated location passes are kept for up to 30 days; disabling location offers deletes the related aggregates.

Storage and external services

GitHub Pages hosts the website. The pilot API and database run on the operator's computer through a Cloudflare HTTPS tunnel. Telegram provides bot interactions, Gmail confirmation and recovery emails, and Stripe the test payment flow. The browser stores diary data, settings and the session; clearing site data removes local copies.

Google Fonts and recognition components loaded from jsDelivr receive browser network information. Maps you open follow the selected map provider's rules. External providers may process data outside Moldova, and their own policies also apply to their services. This web release contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Profiles and records are kept while needed for the account or until deletion. Scheduled database backups run daily and rotate the latest 14 copies. Deleted data may remain in backups that have not yet been replaced. Some technical and payment records may remain for investigating errors, refunds and applicable requirements; we will explain the relevant period and grounds on request.
